Confirmatory Datasets

These datasets provide the strongest evidence. They use plausible values and/or replicate weights for design-aware inference and cover confirmatory-priority traits.

PIAAC Cycle 2headline

Adult skills (literacy, numeracy, adaptive problem solving), ages 16–65 across 10 age bands. PV + replicate weights. 30 headline cells. Contains the strongest positive cell: numeracy at ages 60–65 (VR 1.46×).

ECLS-K:2011headline

Kindergarten cohort followed through 5th grade. Reading and math across 6 waves. Stratified PSU bootstrap. Contains the strongest counterexample: kindergarten reading (VR 0.10×).

PISA 2022headline

Math, reading, science for 15-year-olds. PV + BRR. U.S. sample. 3 headline cells. Math VR 1.30×.

TIMSS 2019headline

Math and science at grades 4 and 8. PV + JRR. 4 headline cells, all male-greater.

TIMSS 2023headline

Same design as TIMSS 2019, 2023 cycle. 4 headline cells, all male-greater.

PIRLS 2021headline

Grade 4 reading. PV + JRR. 3 cells. Overall reading VR near 0.99×—essentially equal variance.

HSLS:09headline

Math achievement, 9th–11th grade. BRR replicate weights. 2 headline cells.

ICILS 2023headline

Computer/information literacy and computational thinking, 8th grade. PV + JRR. 2 headline cells.

Supporting Datasets

These datasets add domain coverage but use simpler inference methods or carry weight-related caveats.

NHANES Selected Cyclessupporting

Physical traits (height, weight, BMI, grip strength), 2011–2023. Design-aware bootstrap. Grip strength consistently male-greater; BMI mixed. Cognition screen QA-only.

NNYFS 2012supporting

Youth physical traits. Design-aware bootstrap. Adds youth-specific physical perspective.

PSID CDS / TASsupporting

Achievement (Woodcock-Johnson, SAT, ACT) and anthropometry across childhood and young adulthood. Simple-design inference. Some TAS rows provisional.

Anchor and Replication Datasets

NLSY datasets serve as replication anchors but use simple SE approximations—method-limited, excluded from headline claims.

NLSY79 Mainmethod-limited

Adult cognition (ASVAB-derived), 2 age bands. Median VR 1.23×, 89% male-greater.

NLSY97 Mainmethod-limited

Adult cognition, 1 age band. Median VR 1.28×, 100% male-greater.

NLSY79 Child and Young Adultprovisional

PPVT, PIAT reading/math, digit span. Some cells rely on fallback maternal weights. Excluded from headline.

Variance Ratio Across Age

Each panel shows how the variance ratio changes across age or grade bands within a dataset. The dashed line at 1.0× marks equal variance.

This project is descriptive, not causal. It does not establish biological or essentialist conclusions.
