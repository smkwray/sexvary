Note on the ECLS-K reading reversal

The fall kindergarten reading cell (VR 0.10×) means male variance is roughly one-tenth of female variance at school entry. By later grades, ECLS-K reading shifts to male-greater variability (VR 1.37× by 4th grade). This developmental reversal is one reason the project does not claim a universal pattern.

Robustness Checks

Each supporting dataset has been compared against unweighted and winsorized variants. Winsorization generally moves results less than dropping survey weights. Sign change rates remain low across all variants.
